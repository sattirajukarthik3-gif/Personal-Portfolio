A global retail and wellness brand was running project cost tracking on a
legacy MS VBA application — brittle, hard to maintain, and disconnected from
modern reporting tools. This engagement modernized it into a React + Python
web platform.

## My role

I led requirements gathering, roadmap planning, and Agile execution for the
rebuild, working closely with engineering to evaluate where AI-assisted
automation could streamline cost analysis and workflow optimization.

## Outcome

The modernized platform's success directly contributed to the client
awarding a Phase 2 engagement — [see the Store-Tracking Modernization
project](/projects/retail-store-tracking-modernization) for that follow-on
work.
