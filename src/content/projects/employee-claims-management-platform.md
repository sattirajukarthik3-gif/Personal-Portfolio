An enterprise insurance client needed a modern claims management platform
capable of pulling in third-party live data feeds to streamline workers'
compensation and liability claims — historically a slow, document-heavy
process.

## My role

I directed delivery of the platform end to end, collaborating with solution
architects on AI-enabled claims processing and intelligent document
workflows. Beyond delivery, I led client communication, prepared the
Statement of Work, and negotiated the commercial terms for a Phase 2
engagement.

## Outcome

A live claims platform that cut down the manual document handling at the
center of the client's claims process, with a Phase 2 already under
negotiation.
