During my internship, I worked as a full-stack developer building a Product
Information Management (PIM) platform for real-time inventory tracking,
collaborating with cross-functional teams to define feature requirements
and release timelines.

## My role

I built the analytics dashboards that sat on top of the PIM data, and
automated the reporting pipelines feeding them — replacing what had been a
manual, error-prone process.

## Outcome

The dashboards improved forecasting accuracy by 25% and the automated
pipelines reduced manual errors while increasing operational speed — my
first real experience owning a feature from requirements through to
measurable impact.
