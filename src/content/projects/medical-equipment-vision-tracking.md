Hospitals lose enormous amounts of staff time physically locating medical
equipment across departments. This project delivered a computer vision
system that scans and identifies equipment in real time, automating what
used to be a manual inventory process.

## My role

I managed cross-functional delivery of the solution end to end — coordinating
engineering, UX, and client stakeholders across globally distributed teams.
That meant running the full Agile cadence: sprint planning, daily stand-ups,
stakeholder communication, weekly demos, and UX discussions, while keeping
the roadmap aligned with the client's operational rollout timeline.

## Outcome

The system now retrieves equipment in under three seconds and cut manual
inventory workload by 95% — turning a recurring operational bottleneck into
a near-instant lookup.
